Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

ToolNest is a set of free browser utilities. This policy explains what happens when you use the site.

Tools and your files

Text, passwords, JSON, images, and other inputs are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. We do not create accounts or store tool output.

Hosting

The static site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data (IP address, user agent, pages requested) as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads, measure them, and personalize them. Google’s use of information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can opt out of personalized ads via Google’s Ads Settings. Visitors in the EEA/UK may see a consent message after we enable AdSense Privacy & messaging.

Cookies

The tools themselves do not set account cookies. Third-party ads (when enabled) may set cookies. We do not sell personal information.
